Chapter 1: The Secret Key

Marry loved the water more than anything else in the world. Every afternoon, when the house grew quiet and her parents fell asleep,
she would lace up her sneakers. Her big golden retriever, Olive, would wait by the back door, his tail thumping softly against the rug.
Marry reached into the flowerpot by the porch and pulled out the cold, brass key to the backyard gate.
They slipped out into the warm sun without making a single sound.
