Chapter 3: The Silver Lake

They finally broke through the trees and stood before Bluebird Lake. The water was as smooth as glass,
reflecting the bright blue sky above. Without waiting a second, Marry kicked off her shoes and revealed the swimsuit
she had hidden under her clothes. Olive let out a soft, excited whine, his eyes locked on the sparkling water.
The lake belonged entirely to them this afternoon.
