The Last Chapter:Caught Red-Handed

A loud bark shattered the silence. Olive, excited by the footsteps, broke free from Marry's grip and ran straight toward the trail.
Marry gasped and jumped out from behind the oak tree, but it was too late. Standing on the path were her parents, who had gone out looking for them.
Her mother looked worried, and her father looked deeply upset. Marry knew her secret was over, and she would be grounded from swimming for the rest of the summer.
