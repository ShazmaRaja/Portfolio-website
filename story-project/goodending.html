The Last Chapter:The Safe Return

The voices slowly faded away as the hikers stayed on the main trail, never noticing the girl and her dog.
Marry let out a huge sigh of relief. She and Olive crawled out of the lake, dried off quickly in the warm sun,
and sneaked back through the gate just in time. As Marry locked the back door, Olive curled up on his rug, completely exhausted.
Their secret was safe for another day, and Marry already couldn't wait for tomorrow.
