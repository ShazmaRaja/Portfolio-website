Chapter 5: The Sudden Noise

Suddenly, Olive froze in the water and his ears perked up. From the distant walking path,
Marry heard the distinct sound of heavy footsteps and loud voices. Someone was coming toward their secret spot.
Panic surged through her as she realized they were about to get caught. She grabbed Olive's collar and quickly guided him behind a large,
fallen oak tree near the shore, holding her breath.
