Chapter 2: The Hidden Path

The path to the lake was completely hidden by tall pine trees and thick bushes. Marry and Olive walked briskly,
keeping their heads down so the neighbors wouldn't see them. Marry felt a thrill of excitement in her chest
as the smell of fresh pine gave way to the crisp scent of lake water. No one in town came to this side of the woods,
which made it the perfect secret hiding spot for an adventure.
