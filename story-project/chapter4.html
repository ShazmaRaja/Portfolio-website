Chapter 4: The Big Splash

With a running leap, Marry dove straight into the cool water. She surfaced with a gasp and a laugh,
shaking the droplets from her eyes. Right behind her, Olive launched himself off the bank with a massive splash.
They swam in circles together, Marry racing ahead and Olive paddling furiously to catch up to her. In the water,
Marry felt completely free, away from rules and chores.
