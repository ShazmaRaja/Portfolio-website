Marry and Olive's Secret Adventure

Some secrets are too good to keep.

For Marry, summer isn't about sitting inside or playing video games. It is about the cool, sparkling water of a hidden lake that no one else knows about.
Together with her loyal dog, Olive. Marry goes on a forbidden journey every single afternoon. Dive into this 6-chapter story to experience the thrill of the perfect hiding spot,
the joy of a secret friendship, and the panic of almost getting caught.
